Questions people ask first

Microservices or a monolith?

Whichever the product needs. AzertyUI's platform is split into services because its domains (absences, expenses, documents, tickets) change independently; a smaller product is often better as one service.

Can you also own the front end?

Yes. On the AzertyUI platform the front end is Angular 21, built by the same team I lead.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the scope, so I quote per project or per day once the scope is written down. The first call and the written scope are free.
